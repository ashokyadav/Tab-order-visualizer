Privacy policy

Tab Order Visualizer, by Ashok Kumar Yadav. Last updated: October 2026.

Short version: the tool collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

What it does with data

Tab Order Visualizer runs entirely inside your own browser, on the one tab where you start it.

  • It has no server, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.
  • It makes no network requests of any kind.
  • It doesn't read or write cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage or any other browser storage.
  • It doesn't keep anything after you close it. Closing it removes everything it added to the page.

Reports you download or copy

When you click Download or Copy, the tool creates a report from the page you're looking at. It includes that page's address, title, and the visible text or labels of the elements it checked. The report is saved to your own computer or clipboard and is never sent anywhere by the tool. If you scan a page that shows personal or confidential information, that information may appear in the report, so treat the report like a screenshot of that page.

Chrome extension permissions

  • activeTab: lets the extension run on the current tab only when you click its button or press its shortcut.
  • scripting: adds the visualizer, which is packaged inside the extension, to that tab.

The extension has no access to any other tab or site, and it doesn't run in the background on pages you visit.

Links

The panel and reports contain a link to the author's LinkedIn profile. Nothing is sent unless you click it, and the link tells your browser not to pass on the address of the page you were on.
